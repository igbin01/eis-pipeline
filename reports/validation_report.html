How the check works

Source
Policastro, S.A. et al. Experimental and Numerical Investigation into the Effect of Water Uptake on the Capacitance of an Organic Coating. Materials 2023, 16, 3623. Table 2 (equivalent-circuit fit values) and their Equation 2 for computed capacitance.
Formula compared
Their Eq. 2, Cc = (Y0·Rpo)1/n / Rpo, is algebraically the Hsu–Mansfeld closed form Q^(1/n) · R^((1−n)/n) already implemented in eispipe.metrics.cpe_to_capacitance. Same equation, derived independently, applied to their own fitted parameters.
What this does not check
The equivalent-circuit fit itself — Rpo, Y0, and n above are taken from the paper, not produced by eispipe's fit_series. A fuller check would digitize the raw Bode data (their Figure 5c,d, same aging condition) and run it through the full pipeline.
t = 0 h outlier
Their Y0 at t = 0 is reported to one significant figure (0.08 × 10−9); an 8% swing from rounding at that precision is expected, not a formula discrepancy.
